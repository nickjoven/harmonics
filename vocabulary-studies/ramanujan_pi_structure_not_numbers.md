# Structure, Not Numbers: Where the Framework Meets Ramanujan's 1/π

## Status / classification

**Class 2 — structural boundary statement (closing synthesis of the
Ramanujan thread).** Tests, and confirms, the hypothesis that the
framework's contact with the Ramanujan 1/π world is in the **structure**
(signature / level / group degrees of freedom) and **never in the
numbers** (the CM discriminant that fixes the integer constants). The
"next path" returns another null at the numerical layer — and the null is
**uniform and structural, not incidental**, which is the informative part.
Retrieval-tier; companion check `ramanujan_pi_structure_not_numbers.py`.
No scorecard claim. Parents: `ramanujan_pi_minimum_alphabet.md`,
`ramanujan_pi_modular_discriminator.md`.

---

## The hypothesis, sharpened

Ramanujan's 1/π series carry **two independent degrees of freedom**:

| DOF | What it is | What it controls |
|---|---|---|
| **STRUCTURE** | the *signature* `r ∈ {2,3,4,6}` (Ramanujan's "theory of elliptic functions to alternative bases", Berndt–Bhargava–Garvan 1995), realized at low levels `ℓ ∈ {1,2,3,(4),(6)}` where the coefficients are clean binomial products | which group / curve / symmetry the series lives on |
| **NUMBERS** | the *CM discriminant* `d` (`τ = √(−d)`) | the specific fast series and its integer constants (`1103, 26390, 9801`; `640320`; …) |

The claim to test: **the framework forces the STRUCTURE DOF and never the
NUMBERS DOF.** The check (`…structure_not_numbers.py`) measures *at what
Farey depth* each datum enters the framework's integers, against the
forced depth (6, where `|F₆| = 13` gives Ω_Λ = 13/19).

---

## Result — both halves, from the check

**STRUCTURE lands inside the forced cone.** The signature set is
`{2,3,4,6}` — exactly the **{2,3}-smooth integers in [2,6]**, equivalently
the crystallographic (lattice-compatible) rotation orders > 1. Every one
is generated by the framework's two forced primes:

    r = 2 = q₂,   r = 3 = q₃,   r = 4 = q₂²,   r = 6 = q₂·q₃ = INTERACT

The framework natively forces `2, 3, 6`; `4 = q₂²` is one multiplication
away. And the realized level is `Γ₀(6)` (`ramanujan_pi_modular_discriminator.md`).
So the **entire structural skeleton of the clean Ramanujan series sits at
the framework's forced depth ≤ 6**, generated by `{2,3}`.

**NUMBERS land outside it — uniformly.** The discriminants of the
*distinctive fast* series:

| series | d | factorization | reach in framework integers |
|---|---|---|---|
| level 2 (1914; 1103/26390) | 58 | 2·**29** | 29 = \|F₉\| — **depth 9** |
| level 1 (Chudnovsky; 640320) | 163 | **163** prime | not a Farey count |
| class-number-3 example | 907 | **907** prime | not a Farey count |
| class-number-4 example | 1555 | 5·**311** | 311 not a Farey count |

None is `{2,3}`-smooth; each needs a prime that is either **genuinely
foreign** to the Farey hierarchy (67, 163, 311, …) or enters only as a
**deep** Farey count (29 = |F₉|, 43 = |F₁₁|) — three to five levels past
the forced cone, in the "z-fighting / far-plane" regime
`minimum_alphabet.md`'s cluster note defines and declines.

**The honest caveat that makes the verdict precise.** Some *small* CM
discriminants ARE framework integers — the Heegner numbers `2, 3, 7, 11,
19` are `|F₁|, |F₂|, |F₄|, |F₅|, |F₇|`, all in the forced cone. But these
are exactly the **slow, structurally-simple** series (small constants);
they are the structural end of the discriminant axis. The moment one asks
for the **distinctive numbers** — the large constants that made the 1914
formula famous — the required discriminant jumps to deep/foreign primes.
So the split is not "framework integers vs not," it is **forced-depth
structure vs far-plane arithmetic**, and the famous numbers live strictly
on the far plane.

## Verdict — your hypothesis, confirmed and made precise

> **The framework forces the *shape* of Ramanujan's 1/π series and none of
> its *coordinates*.** Its two primes `{2,3}` generate the structural
> degrees of freedom in full — the signature set `{2,3,4,6}`, the level
> `Γ₀(6)`, the cusp/sector lattice — because those are the `{2,3}`-smooth /
> crystallographic / genus-zero modular data, which live at the forced
> depth ≤ 6. It generates **no** discriminant: the CM moduli that fix the
> distinctive integer constants require primes off the `{2,3}` hierarchy or
> Farey depths past the framework's far plane.

This is exactly the "3-or-4-formulas" intuition. The clean families ARE the
**four signatures `{2,3,4,6}`** — a small, structural set the framework
covers with its two primes — and each carries a *tower* of CM discriminants
(the numbers) reaching progressively deeper, which the framework cannot
resolve. **Another null at the numerical layer, and it is telling:** it is
not that the framework *missed* the numbers, it is that structure and
numbers are *different degrees of freedom*, and the framework's machinery —
forcing small-prime / genus-zero modular structure — is constitutionally a
structure-fixer, not a moduli-fixer.

This is also the **common cause**, not a prediction: both the framework and
the clean part of Ramanujan's theory concentrate on `{2,3}`-smooth modular
structure for the *same* underlying reason (genus-zero levels, widest
Arnold tongues / lattice-compatible orders dominate). The shared `{2,3}`
home is real and explains the recurring near-miss; it does not promote to
the framework deriving π, because deriving any specific 1/π series means
choosing a discriminant, and **choosing discriminants is the one move the
framework's level-only, depth-bounded machinery structurally cannot make.**

## Where the thread closes

The three notes form one arc:
1. `ramanujan_pi_minimum_alphabet.md` — the formula decomposes into Z +
   one parabola (√2) + a completion limit; π is a completion artifact.
2. `ramanujan_pi_modular_discriminator.md` — the framework forces the
   **level** `Γ₀(6)` (home shared) but no **discriminant** (address
   disjoint).
3. **this note** — generalizing across the signature families: the
   framework forces the **structural** DOF `{2,3,4,6}`/`Γ₀(6)` uniformly,
   and the **numerical** DOF (CM discriminants) never. Structure, not
   numbers — by construction.

The only thing that would reopen it: a substrate-side mechanism that
selects a *specific* CM discriminant from framework integers at the forced
depth — which, per this note, the machinery is the wrong *type* to do.
