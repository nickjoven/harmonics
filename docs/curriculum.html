The curriculum, from a wave outward

A reader-facing teaching arc built inward: it starts from phenomena a reader can follow with no prior math and arrives at the framework's named objects from the other side. Every module is a consequence of the one before it, and the whole spine grows outward from a single object — a wave. History and naming come after the construction; the name is the reward, not the prerequisite. Written modules link to their source; run each one's engine.py to watch the construction work.
