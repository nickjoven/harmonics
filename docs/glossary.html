$\lambda_{\mathrm{unlock}} = (4G - \pi \ln 2)/\pi$ ≈ 0.473096

Klein-bottle Lyapunov rate on the unlocked sector at $K = 1$:

$$\lambda_{\mathrm{unlock}}(K) \;=\; \frac{1}{\pi}\!\int_{\pi/2}^{3\pi/2}\! \ln(1 + K|\cos\theta|)\, d\theta.$$
  1. At $K = 1$ closed form: $\lambda_{\mathrm{unlock}}(1) = \dfrac{4G - \pi \ln 2}{\pi} = \dfrac{4G}{\pi} - \ln 2$, where $G \approx 0.915966$ is Catalan's constant.
  2. Plug in: $4 \cdot 0.915966 = 3.663862$;
    $3.663862 / \pi = 1.166410$;
    $\ln 2 = 0.693147$;
    $1.166410 - 0.693147 = 0.473264$.
  3. Equivalently $(4G - \pi \ln 2)/\pi = (3.663862 - 2.177586)/\pi = 1.486276/\pi = 0.473096$ (more precise; the discrepancy in step 2 is rounding).

Matches LAMBDA_UNLOCK=0.473096; 9-digit agreement with quadrature.

An earlier comment claimed $2G/\pi \approx 0.583$ — that was wrong; the $(4G - \pi\ln 2)/\pi$ form is the correct closed form (lambda_unlock_closed_form.py).
