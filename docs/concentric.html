Why “extremely stable at our frequency”

The two gold nodes on the observation ring are walls — the smallest scale (Planck) and the largest scale (Hubble) at which a coherent wave can exist at all. Between them is a band of many decades of wavelength, and our local physics — sound, light, chemistry, the biology that runs you — lives deep inside that band.

The green region in the center marks that band visually. We are far from both walls; no boundary correction matters at our scale; the wave construction at the center describes what we observe without modification. The framework's dramatic claims happen at the walls — not at our position, but at the boundary scales where the construction first breaks down. Locally, the behavior is stable because it is heavily reinforced from below and from above.

Reading the tiers

Not every familiar observation in the ring is something the framework derives from substrate. The legend at lower-left distinguishes three derivation tiers, and each link in the diagram carries its tier in its edge style and the framework-node fill:

  • T1 (derived) — solid edges, filled framework nodes. The framework constructs the observation (or a clean substrate analog) from its primitives. Time dilation, Planck wall, Hubble wall survive this bar: Einstein's equations fall out of the Kuramoto continuum limit at K=1, and the Planck/Hubble ratio is derived to 0.48%.
  • T2 (engaged) — dashed edges, hollow framework nodes. The framework engages with the observation or makes predictions parametric in it, but does not derive the observation itself. Redshift and E = mc² sit here: the framework consumes z as a coordinate and derives mass ratios, but neither the cosmic stretch nor the inertia↔energy equivalence is constructed from substrate.
  • T3 (imported) — dotted edges, dashed-outline nodes. A textbook landmark with no constructive substrate engagement. Doppler alone falls in this bracket: the framework's intra-substrate frequency arithmetic (FM beat / CRT composition) is structurally different from observer-motion Doppler. Kept here in honesty about the gap, not pruned to make the diagram look fuller.

Reading the legend, the visitor sees not only what the framework claims about familiar physics but how strongly each claim is anchored. The pattern itself is part of the message: when something is derived from substrate, the framework says so by drawing a solid line. When it isn't, the dashed or dotted edges are the framework being honest about its own perimeter.
