## The dependence chain

The argument is a four-step structural composition.

**Step 1 — Planck scale = N=3 self-sustenance threshold.**
`planck_scale.md` derives the Planck scale as the minimum scale
where the SL(2,ℝ) coupling loop closes. The Stribeck lattice
shows N=2 fails to self-sustain (linear passthrough,
P(ω₀)/P(ω_d) ≈ 0.06); N=3 just crosses over (P ≈ 1.03); N≥4
strengthens. The Iwasawa decomposition SL(2,ℝ) = K·A·N exhibits
three structurally distinct coupling stages (phase/amplitude/
detuning); killing any one collapses the loop. Below the Planck
scale, "the mean field cannot constitute itself"
(`planck_scale.md` L55).

**Step 2 — Substrate ≡ field dynamics on K².**
The framework's substrate is not Klein-bottle topology in
isolation; it is the sine-Gordon field configuration φ on K².
`q_mod2_conservation_theorem.md` opens "Let K² = [0,L_x] × [0,L_y]
be the Klein bottle" and proceeds with φ defined on it. K² is
the **field arena** — the structural setting the substrate
dynamics realize, not an external mathematical object the
substrate happens to reference.

**Step 3 — Field existence requires self-sustenance.**
A field that cannot constitute itself is not a substrate fact.
Composing Steps 1 and 2: below the Planck threshold, the field
does not exist as a self-sustaining dynamical object;
consequently, K² is not realized as a substrate fact (only as
an external mathematical object the framework's apparatus is no
longer pointed at).

**Step 4 — Q mod 2 well-definedness requires K² as substrate
fact.** Q mod 2 is defined via the antiperiodic-cycle topology
of K² acting on field configurations (the kink-number reduction
of `q_mod2_conservation_theorem.md` Step 1, eqs. 1–4). Without
K² as a substrate fact, there is no antiperiodic-cycle structure
acting on a substrate field; Q mod 2 has no referent.

**Composition.** Planck threshold (N≥3) → substrate self-sustains
→ field φ exists on K² → K² topology is realized as substrate
fact → Q mod 2 well-defined. The chain runs in one direction;
its first link (Planck self-sustenance) and its last link (Q mod
2 well-definedness) co-determine each other through the substrate
chain.

This is the structural identity: **Klein-bottle topology
emergence ≡ Planck-scale self-sustenance**.

## The fuzzy floor

The Planck "floor" is honestly fuzzy, not a sharp boundary, and
the fuzziness has a specific mechanism in each composing piece.

**Stribeck threshold is a crossover, not a discontinuity.**
P(ω₀)/P(ω_d) is 0.06 at N=2, 1.03 at N=3, 1.43 at N=4. The N=3
crossover is the *minimum* self-sustenance — the coupling loop
*just* closes. There is no abrupt transition; the ratio increases
continuously with N. This is the framework's own characterization
(`planck_scale.md` L11-28).

**Born rule degrades, not cuts off.** Per `planck_scale.md`
L253-255: P = |ψ|² "holds for scales >> l_P; *degrades* at scale
~ l_P; undefined below l_P." The Born rule does not switch off
at l_P; it loses fidelity across a crossover regime around l_P.

**Q mod 2 inherits the crossover.** Via the structural identity,
the Q mod 2 well-definedness threshold is the same crossover as
substrate self-sustenance and Born rule degradation. Q mod 2 is
not preserved-or-dissolved on either side of a sharp boundary;
it emerges *across* the crossover, with intermediate scales
giving intermediate well-definedness.

This is the honest reframing of the Planck-scale Q mod 2
contingency: the Planck floor is the regime where the substrate
is constituting itself, where K² is becoming a substrate fact,
and where Q mod 2 is emerging as a well-defined invariant. None
of these have sharp on/off transitions. The "floor" is the
*crossover regime*, not a wall.

## What this collapses

The conservation stratification audit (`L336-432`) listed three
candidate Planck-scale Q mod 2 resolutions:

- **(a) Q mod 2 dissolves**: if Planck-scale substrate is
  non-topological, the concept of "encircling the antiperiodic
  direction" is not well-defined; Q mod 2 becomes meaningless.
- **(b) Conserved by inability**: at sub-L_x scales (which
  include Planck), the diameter condition `< L_x` is trivially
  satisfied, so the theorem holds vacuously.
- **(c) Algebraic re-expression**: an algebraic Z/2 invariant
  not requiring smooth topology might exist and could persist
  where the topological reading dissolves.

The structural identity picks **(a) by derivation**. Below the
Planck threshold, no substrate constitutes itself; no field φ
exists on K²; K² is not realized as a substrate fact; the
antiperiodic-cycle topology has no referent for Q mod 2 to read.
(a) is not a guess about non-topological Planck physics — it is
the consequence of K² requiring the substrate to self-sustain
in order to be a substrate fact at all.

**(b) is the theorem's correct statement of its own scale of
applicability, but the wrong physical picture of what happens
at Planck.** The theorem covers processes of support `< L_x`;
sub-Planck processes satisfy this trivially; therefore the
theorem's preservation result holds vacuously. This is correct
about what the theorem proves. But what the theorem proves is
that local-by-its-own-criterion processes preserve Q mod 2 — a
statement about the theorem's coverage, not about substrate
behavior at Planck. The two are not in tension; they are
statements about different things. The audit's contribution is
to keep them distinct.

**(c) is orthogonal.** The question "is there an algebraic Z/2
invariant that doesn't require smooth topology" is independent
of the Planck-emergence question. (c)'s success or failure
doesn't change the structural identity; it would only change
*what other Z/2 invariants the framework can name* in regimes
where the topological one is unavailable. Flagged as the next
productive thread; not pursued here.

## Empirical alignment

The audit's verdict is about framework structure, not about
direct experimental access to sub-Planck physics. But three
independently-tested empirical observations align with the
framing — at different strengths, by different mechanisms.

### Microscopic black hole hunts (weak alignment — consistency)

ATLAS and CMS searches for thermal-spectrum microscopic black
holes at the LHC have set semi-classical bounds at approximately
9–10 TeV (model-dependent across number of extra dimensions).
These constrain low-scale-gravity scenarios (large extra
dimensions per Arkani-Hamed-Dimopoulos-Dvali; warped extra
dimensions per Randall-Sundrum) that would place the effective
Planck scale at TeV-accessible energies.

Null results favor the standard Planck scale (~10¹⁹ GeV),
consistent with the framework's N=3 threshold sitting at the
standard Planck scale and not being smeared down by extra-
dimensional access. The framework predicts no LHC-accessible
substrate transitions; LHC sees no such transitions. This is
consistency, not corroboration — neither prediction is sharply
distinguished from a wide range of standard-Planck-scale BSM
scenarios.

### Higgs boson (substantive structural alignment)

The Higgs at 125 GeV combined with the measured top mass places
the Standard Model vacuum at the edge of metastability. The
Higgs quartic self-coupling λ runs to near-zero at the Planck
scale per Standard Model RG flow, meaning the SM is "marginally
self-consistent up to Planck."

This structurally parallels the Stribeck threshold's
P(ω₀)/P(ω_d) ≈ 1.03 at N=3 — also marginal self-sustenance.
Two independent pictures of "Planck-scale physics is just barely
self-consistent," each from a different starting point (RG flow
of SM couplings vs. Stribeck synchronization threshold).

This is more than non-contradiction. Both pictures share the
*crossover-not-cutoff shape* that the audit's fuzzy-floor
framing identifies as the honest description of the Planck
regime. The Higgs marginal-metastability is the SM's
parallel-language expression of the same crossover structure
the framework derives from N=3 self-sustenance. Whether the two
pictures are derivationally related or independent expressions
of a deeper structural feature is open; the alignment of their
shapes is empirically established.

### Entanglement bounds (strongest alignment — framework floor)

Bell-test experiments saturate the Tsirelson bound (2√2 in CHSH)
and never exceed it. Post-quantum correlations of the PR-box
type — which would saturate the algebraic bound of 4 — have not
been observed at any scale tested.

This is the framework's empirical floor. The K² single-
antiperiodic-cycle structure forces a single complex structure
J on the substrate (`complex_amplitude_uniqueness.md`); the
single-J apparatus produces ℂ-QM with Tsirelson saturation, not
the looser ℍ structure that would admit super-Tsirelson
correlations. ℍ-QM is excluded empirically because we observe
ℂ-QM's saturation pattern, not ℍ-QM's; the exclusion mechanism
is precisely the entanglement bound observation.

The discrete-lossless / quantum-lossy reading
(`primitives_vs_addresses_candidate.md`) lands here: lossy
projection onto a single ℂ phase produces Tsirelson saturation.
The looser ℍ structure would require independent R-action,
which the apparatus doesn't supply (per the third-caveat
generative-reading verdict) — and which, importantly, the
empirical entanglement observation also doesn't supply.
Apparatus and empiricism agree on the constraint.

### What alignment shows and doesn't

Three independent empirical results, three different energy
regimes (LHC TeV, SM-up-to-Planck running, all-scale entanglement
tests), three different mechanisms (collider physics, vacuum
stability, Bell-type correlation tests). Each is consistent with
the audit's verdict; entanglement bounds saturate-but-don't-
exceed in the way the framework's single-J apparatus predicts.

Alignment is not derivation. The Planck-scale itself is not
directly probed; the structural-identity claim is about framework
structure, not about experimental access. What the empirical
results corroborate is the *consistency* of the framework's
fuzzy-floor framing with what is observed at the scales we *can*
probe — and the absence of any observation that would falsify the
framing (no extra-dimensional Planck, no super-Tsirelson
correlations, no anomalous Higgs decays indicating sub-Planck
new physics).

and is defined independently of K² topology?

Candidate sources of algebraic Z/2:

1. **The XOR rule.** `klein_bottle.md` L141-152 (per
   `klein_z2_decomposition_falsifier_2.md`'s reading) carries the
   algebraic mode-pairing rule `p_x + p_y ≡ 1 (mod 2)`. This is
   already algebraic; the question is whether the XOR-Z/2 is the
   *same* Z/2 as Q mod 2's topological-winding parity, or a

   Mihailescu-forced `(q_2, q_3) = (2, 3)` carries Z/2

   2 — or to a different framework-level Z/2 — is not currently
   derived.

3. **Sub-Planck Z/2 from a categorical or fixed-point source.**
   Speculative; no candidate yet named in the apparatus.

Downstream cascade if (c) lands: depending on the candidate, this
could touch `complex_amplitude_uniqueness.md`'s ℂ-uniqueness
derivation, `born_rule_tongues.py`'s Born rule derivation, and

The (a)/(b) branch order from the stratification audit applies
