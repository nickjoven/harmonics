# Comparison Class

A methodological orientation for the synchronization-cost framework. Identifies what the framework claims, what it does not claim, and which class of programs it should be evaluated against.

## The Standard Model is not the comparison

The Standard Model is a catalog plus a calculus. A list of particles, a list of couplings, a list of mixings, and a renormalizable QFT machine that turns those lists into measurements to ten significant figures. Internally consistent. Empirically extraordinary. And silent on every question of the form *why these and not others*.

The silence is policy, not oversight. From Wightman onward, the QFT tradition explicitly disclaimed the question. "Shut up and calculate" was not sarcasm — it was a research-program decision to defer ontology.

This framework is not in competition with the Standard Model. It is answering questions the Standard Model deferred.

## The right comparison class

The framework's peers are unification programs that explicitly claim the Standard Model's parameters should not be free:

- Grand Unified Theories (SU(5), SO(10), E₈)
- String theory and its compactifications
- Loop quantum gravity
- Causal set theory
- Asymptotic safety
- Wolfram's hypergraph program
- Geometric Unity

Each accepts an empirical burden the Standard Model is exempt from: produce numbers the Standard Model only catalogs. Membership in this class is a commitment, not a claim.

## What the framework claims

Derived rather than measured:

- Ω_Λ = 13 / 19 ≈ 0.6842 — the dark-energy fraction, from Farey-partition counting at depth 6
- sin² θ_W = 8 / 35 ≈ 0.2286 — the Weinberg angle, from duty-cycle ratios on the Klein bottle
- α_s / α₂ = 27 / 8 = 3.375 — gauge-coupling ratio at high scale
- n_s ≈ 0.9649 — spectral tilt, from devil's-staircase self-similarity at φ⁻¹
- d = 3 — spatial dimension, from dim SL(2, ℝ)
- Three generations — from a counting result on Farey adjacency
- Charge quantization — from compactness of S¹
- Conservation laws — as compactness of the configuration space

Anchors rather than constants of nature:

- ℏ, G, c — collapse to one Planck scale; a coordinate on the tree, not a parameter of physics
- v = 246 GeV — the dimensionful conversion to particle-physics units
- H₀ — our address on the tree at depth ≈ 146

Categorical claims rather than catalogued distinctions:

- Matter / force, fermion / boson, quark / lepton are Farey-level distinctions on a single substrate. Not primitives.

## The natural test

A unification program's empirical test is to produce numbers the Standard Model leaves uncomputed, with derivations rather than fits. This is not an unfair test. It is the test every member of the comparison class signed up for.

So far, four nontrivial dimensionless ratios fall out as exact rationals (13/19, 8/35, 27/8, and n_s to four significant figures), plus the d = 3 forcing and the charge-quantization argument. None were targets of fit. All follow structural derivations. That is what counts as traction.

The same test cuts both ways. A future framework prediction that misses observation is genuine falsification. A successor framework that derives a fifth or sixth ratio with the same machinery is genuine corroboration. Either is informative — which is what makes the test natural.

## Why underclaiming is a mistake

Modesty about ambition is corrosive when the ambition is genuine. Three reasons not to soft-pedal:

1. **The deferred questions are real.** *Why exactly three dimensions? Why Ω_Λ ≈ 0.685 and not 0.3? Why three generations?* These are not nuisance questions. They are the unanswered residue of a century of physics. Pretending the framework is "just describing" them obscures that it is deriving them.

2. **The right comparison invites the right scrutiny.** If the framework claims unification, reviewers will demand the consequences of unification be tested. That demand sharpens the program. Underclaiming substitutes "hard to falsify" for "ambitious to verify" — which lets the program drift instead of growing.

3. **The successes are conditional on the claim.** Ω_Λ = 13/19 is interesting because the framework was attempting to derive it. As a coincidence, it would be unremarkable. As a derivation that fits Farey counting from first principles, it is structural evidence. Without the claim, the evidence collapses.

## The category error to guard against

A student or reviewer who treats the framework as competing with the Standard Model on QED's home turf has made the wrong comparison. The framework does not improve on g − 2. It does not improve on cross-section calculations. It does not improve on Standard-Model precision tests — because none of those quantities were the target.

The Standard Model produced a catalog of numbers that need explaining. The framework explains a subset of them. The remainder is the next test.

## What follows from this orientation

Several of the framework's odder-sounding claims become natural rather than provocative once the comparison class is fixed:

- *ℏ is an anchor, not a constant of nature* — natural, because the framework derives Standard-Model inputs, and ℏ is one
- *d = 3 is forced* — natural, same reason
- *Charge quantization is a counting result* — natural, same reason
- *Fermions and bosons are Farey levels of one substrate* — natural, same reason
- *The cosmological constant is not fine-tuned; it is a counting fact* — natural, same reason

Without the comparison class made explicit, all five sound like crank physics. With it, they are the natural consequences of a different research program.

## Status

Conceptual and methodological orientation. Cited from FRAMEWORK.md as context for any reader who asks whether the framework is competing with the Standard Model. The answer is no — it is in a different conversation. The Standard Model deferred a class of questions. The framework answers them. Whether the answers are correct is a separate matter, decided by the empirical record on dimensionless ratios the Standard Model only catalogs.

The framework's bet is that the deferred questions had structural answers all along. The empirical record so far is consistent with that bet.
